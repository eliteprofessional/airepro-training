# Account Management

Keep your Airepro account secure and up to date. This guide covers login details, password changes, notification preferences, and how to request account closure.

## Sign-in options

You can sign in with:

- Email and password
- A one-time link sent to your verified email (when enabled for your environment)

If you use multiple devices, sign out of shared computers when you finish.

## Change your password

1. Open **Account** → **Security**.
2. Choose **Change password**.
3. Enter your current password, then your new password twice.
4. Save. You may be asked to sign in again on other devices.

Password tips:

- Use at least 12 characters
- Mix letters, numbers, and symbols
- Do not reuse passwords from other sites

## Update email or phone

- **Email**: change requires confirmation on the new address before it becomes primary.
- **Phone**: used for alerts and recovery when SMS is enabled for your account.

Until the new email is confirmed, the previous address remains active for login.

## Notification preferences

Control what Airepro sends you:

| Channel | Typical use | Can disable? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Applications, billing, security | Some security messages stay on |
| In-app | Status updates, reminders | Yes |
| SMS | Time-sensitive alerts | Yes, if configured |

## Sessions and devices

From **Security** → **Active sessions** you can:

- Review recent sign-ins by device and location
- End a session you do not recognize
- Force sign-out everywhere after a password reset

## Close or deactivate your account

If you need to leave Airepro:

1. Download any documents you still need (applications, receipts).
2. Cancel an active subscription first if applicable.
3. Request account closure from **Account** → **Danger zone**.

Closure is permanent after the retention window described in your terms of service.

## Need help?

If you are locked out or see unfamiliar activity, contact support with the approximate time of the issue and the email on the account. Do not share your password in any message.
