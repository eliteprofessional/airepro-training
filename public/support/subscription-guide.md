# Subscription Guide

Airepro subscriptions unlock premium tools such as advanced matching, priority visibility, and team features. This guide explains plans, billing, upgrades, and cancellations.

## Plan overview

Plans vary by market. In general you will see:

- **Free / Basic** — core profile and limited applications
- **Pro** — expanded tools for active job seekers
- **Team / Brokerage** — multi-seat management (when enabled)

Exact names and prices appear on the billing page for your account.

## Start a subscription

1. Open **Billing** or **Subscription** from your account menu.
2. Select a plan and billing interval (monthly or annual).
3. Enter payment details through the secure checkout.
4. Confirm. Access updates as soon as payment succeeds.

You will receive a receipt by email. Keep it for your records.

## Manage billing

From the billing area you can:

- View current plan and renewal date
- Update card or payment method
- Download invoices and receipts
- Switch between monthly and annual (proration rules apply)

### Example invoice reference

```text
Invoice: INV-2026-00482
Plan:    Pro (Annual)
Period:  2026-08-26 → 2027-08-25
Status:  Paid
```

## Upgrades and downgrades

| Change | When it takes effect | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Upgrade | Usually immediately | You may be charged a prorated amount |
| Downgrade | Often at period end | Confirm on the confirmation screen |
| Seat add (teams) | Immediately | Billing adjusts for added seats |

## Cancel a subscription

1. Open **Billing** → **Cancel subscription**.
2. Confirm the end date of access.
3. Optionally share feedback so we can improve the product.

You typically keep access until the end of the paid period. Auto-renew turns off after cancellation.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails:

- Update your payment method promptly
- Retry from the billing page
- Watch for emails about grace periods

Unresolved failures may pause premium features until payment succeeds.

## Refunds and questions

Refund eligibility follows the policy linked from checkout and your terms of service. For billing disputes, include the invoice number and approximate charge date when you contact support.
