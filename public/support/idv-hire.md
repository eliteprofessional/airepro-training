# Hire Identity Verification (IDV)

Identity Verification (IDV) confirms who you are so you can use Hire features that require a trusted profile—such as applying to roles, unlocking marketplace actions, and completing payouts.

**Where to open it in Hire**

1. Sign in to Airepro Hire.
2. Go to **Settings → Identity Verification**.
3. Or open: `/dashboard/settings/identity_verification`

Estimated total time: **15–20 minutes** if documents and a quiet space for the live check are ready.

---

## Before you start

- Complete steps **in order**—later stages stay locked until earlier ones are approved.
- Keep government ID and supporting documents nearby.
- Use a **stable internet** connection.
- Names should **match across documents and your Hire profile**.
- Use clear photos: no glare, no crop that hides edges or text, no expired IDs.

---

## Paths by role

### Intern or Freelancer

1. Identity Verification (PAN & Aadhaar)
2. Education Verification (10th certificate identification / roll number)
3. Skill Evaluation
4. Bank Verification
5. Visual Verification (document upload + liveness / Airepro Meet)

### Client or TSM

1. Authorized person identity (PAN, Aadhaar last 4, designation)
2. Business Verification
3. Bank Verification
4. Visual Verification (document upload + liveness / Airepro Meet)

---

## Step-by-step instructions

### 1. Identity / authorized person

**Intern / Freelancer — Identity Verification**

- Submit **PAN** and **Aadhaar** details as prompted.
- Ensure documents are valid and not expired.
- Photos should be clear and legible; avoid glare.
- Names should match across all documents.

**Client / TSM — Authorized person identity**

- These are the **authorized person’s** IDs (not the company’s).
- Enter PAN, Aadhaar last 4 digits, and designation.
- Use the authorized signatory’s documents.
- Company registration details come in the next stage.

Tips:

- Estimated time: about **2–4 minutes**
- Double-check spelling against the physical ID

### 2. Education or business

**Intern / Freelancer — Education Verification**

- Enter the identification or roll number from your **10th certificate**.
- Use the number **exactly as printed**, including hyphens or slashes.
- Do **not** upload a certificate file unless the form explicitly asks for one.

Tips:

- Estimated time: about **3–4 minutes**

**Client / TSM — Business Verification**

- Confirm the legal entity with registration details and company proof.
- Use the company’s **legal registered name**.
- **GSTIN** is required unless you are a GST-exempt proprietor.
- **CIN / LLPIN** is required for Pvt Ltd, LLP, and companies.
- Upload GST certificate, Certificate of Incorporation, or an authorization letter as requested.

Tips:

- Estimated time: about **4–6 minutes**

### 3. Skill Evaluation (Intern / Freelancer only)

- Complete the technical and related skill assessments shown in the wizard.
- The technical test typically has **20 MCQ** questions.
- Complete tests in **one sitting**.
- Use a stable internet connection.
- Retake may be available after **7 days** if you do not pass.

Tips:

- Estimated time: about **15–20 minutes**

### 4. Bank Verification

- Provide an **active** bank account for payouts.
- Prefer an account in your name (talent) or the company / authorized payee name (client).
- Upload a **cancelled cheque** or bank statement when asked.
- Double-check account numbers before submit.

Tips:

- Estimated time: about **2–3 minutes**

### 5. Visual Verification

Visual verification has two parts: document upload and a live identity check.

#### Document upload

- Answer whether you are visually challenged.
- If **Yes**, upload a clear disability document (JPG or PNG).
- If **No**, continue to the liveness check.
- Ensure good lighting and no glare; names on the document should match your profile when present.

#### Liveness check (Airepro Meet)

- Book your **Airepro Meet** slot when prompted.
- Test camera and microphone before joining.
- Use a quiet, well-lit room with stable internet.
- Keep your **government ID** ready for the video call.
- Join a few minutes early.
- After booking, use **Refresh** on the verification page if status does not update immediately.
- Watch for a confirmation email with the meeting link; if email fails, the meeting link on the page may still be valid—refresh and open it from Hire.

Tips:

- Estimated time: about **5–15 minutes** including booking and the call

## Verification statuses

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not started | Step not submitted | Open the step and complete the form |
| Pending | Submitted; waiting for review or live check | Wait; refresh after Meet booking |
| Approved | Step accepted | Continue to the next unlocked step |
| Declined | Needs changes | Read the reason, fix documents, and resubmit |

Progress on the Identity Verification page reflects how many required stages are approved.

---

## After you book liveness

1. Keep the Meet link from Hire or email.
2. Join a few minutes early with camera and mic working.
3. Have your government ID in hand.
4. Return to **Settings → Identity Verification** and refresh status after the session.
5. Complete any remaining declined steps until all required stages show **Approved**.

---

## Troubleshooting

**Upload fails**

- Use JPG, PNG, or PDF as allowed by the form.
- Reduce file size if the upload is rejected.
- Retry on another browser; temporarily disable aggressive blockers.

**Stuck on Pending**

- Confirm you finished the Meet / live check if that step is open.
- Refresh the Identity Verification page.
- Check email (including spam) for Meet confirmation or reviewer requests.

**Declined — resubmit required**

- Read the decline reason carefully.
- Replace only the flagged documents unless instructed otherwise.
- Ensure names match your profile and other IDs.

**Cannot open later stages**

- Earlier dependencies must be **Approved** first (for example, identity before education, bank before visual).

**Session expired or unauthorized on IDV**

- Sign out and sign back into Hire, then reopen Identity Verification.
- Do not complete Meet booking in a private window without being logged into Hire.

**Meet link missing**

- Refresh scheduled interview status on the Visual / Liveness step.
- Check whether a confirmation email already sent the link.
- Contact support if the booking shows scheduled but no link appears.
