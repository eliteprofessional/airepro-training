# Getting Started with Airepro

Welcome to **Airepro** — the platform that helps real estate professionals find opportunities, manage profiles, and stay connected with hiring teams.

This guide walks you through your first session so you can create an account, complete your profile, and start applying.

## What you need

- A valid email address
- A modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari)
- About 10–15 minutes for initial setup

## Create your account

1. Open the Airepro sign-up page.
2. Enter your email and choose a strong password.
3. Confirm your email using the link we send you.
4. Sign in and land on your dashboard.

> Tip: Use a work email if your brokerage asks you to. You can update contact details later in Account Management.

## Complete your profile basics

After sign-in, finish these steps before applying to roles:

- Add your full name and preferred display name
- Upload a professional photo
- Select your markets and license status
- Add a short bio (2–3 sentences is enough to start)

## Your first application

When your profile shows as ready:

1. Browse open opportunities from the Jobs or Opportunities area.
2. Open a listing and review requirements.
3. Click **Apply** and follow any verification prompts.
4. Track status from your applications list.

## Quick reference

| Step | Where | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign up | Auth / Register | Account created |
| Verify email | Inbox link | Login unlocked |
| Profile basics | Profile editor | Eligible to apply |
| Apply | Opportunity detail | Application submitted |

## Example profile URL pattern

Some share links look like this:

```text
https://app.airepro.example/profile/your-slug
```

Replace `your-slug` with the public handle shown on your profile settings page.
