# Frequently Asked Questions

Short answers to common Airepro questions. For deeper walkthroughs, open the full guides linked below.

## General

### What is Airepro?

Airepro is a platform for real estate hiring and professional profiles. Candidates build verified profiles; teams post opportunities and review applicants.

### Is there a mobile app?

Support and feature availability depend on your deployment. Most workflows work in a modern mobile browser. Check your organization for any native apps.

### Who can see my profile?

Visibility depends on your privacy and verification settings. Public fields may appear to hiring teams; private documents stay restricted to review workflows.

## Account and access

### I never got a verification email

- Check spam and promotions folders
- Confirm you typed the email correctly at sign-up
- Use **Resend verification** on the sign-in screen
- Wait a few minutes; some providers delay delivery

### Can I change my username or public slug?

Yes, from profile settings when the handle is available. Changing it may break old share links.

### How do I reset my password?

Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. We email a reset link to your verified address.

## Applications and verification

### Why can’t I apply to a role?

Common reasons:

1. Profile basics are incomplete
2. Verification is required and not yet approved
3. The listing is closed or outside your market
4. Your subscription tier does not include that feature

### How long does verification take?

Review times vary. Check the status table on [Profile Verification](/support/profile-verification) and any emails from the review team.

### Can I edit an application after submitting?

Some listings allow updates before review starts; others lock after submit. Open the application detail page to see available actions.

## Billing

### Will I be charged automatically?

Yes, if auto-renew is on for an active paid plan. Cancel before the renewal date to stop the next charge. See the [Subscription Guide](/support/subscription-guide).

### Where are my invoices?

Open **Billing** → **Invoices** (or **Receipts**) to download PDFs for paid periods.
