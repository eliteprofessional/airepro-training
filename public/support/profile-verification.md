Verification builds trust between candidates, freelancers, clients, and hiring teams on Airepro Hire. Completing it unlocks applications, marketplace actions, and payouts that require a verified profile.

> Open **Settings → Identity Verification** in Hire, or go to `/dashboard/settings/identity_verification`.

## Why verification matters

- Confirms you are who you say you are
- Reduces fraud and duplicate accounts
- Helps recruiters and clients move faster with confidence
- Unlocks steps that stay gated until earlier stages are approved

## What you will complete

Depending on your role, Hire guides you through a staged wizard:

### Intern or freelancer

1. Identity (PAN & Aadhaar)
2. Education (10th certificate identification)
3. Skill evaluation
4. Bank verification
5. Visual verification and liveness (Airepro Meet)

### Client or TSM

1. Authorized person identity
2. Business verification
3. Bank verification
4. Visual verification and liveness

For a full walkthrough of every IDV step, see [Hire Identity Verification (IDV)](/support/idv-hire).

## Before you submit

- Complete stages **in order** — later steps stay locked until earlier ones are approved
- Keep government ID and supporting documents ready
- Use clear photos: no glare, no heavy crop, no expired IDs
- Make sure names match across documents and your Hire profile
- Prefer a stable connection; plan **15–20 minutes** end to end

## Verification statuses

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Not started | Step not submitted | Open the stage and complete the form |
| Pending | Waiting for review or live check | Wait; refresh after Meet booking |
| Approved | Stage accepted | Continue to the next unlocked step |
| Declined | Needs changes | Fix the flagged items and resubmit |

## How to start

1. Sign in to Airepro Hire.
2. Go to **Settings → Identity Verification**.
3. Follow the active stage until it shows **Approved**.
4. Move through remaining stages until the wizard is complete.
5. After liveness, refresh status and keep your Meet confirmation handy.

## After you are verified

- Keep bank and identity details current
- Re-submit if a stage is declined or documents expire
- Update business or authorized-person details when your company changes
- Re-verify if your legal name changes

## Troubleshooting

**Upload fails**

- Use JPEG, PNG, or PDF under the size limit shown on the form
- Try another browser or temporarily disable aggressive ad blockers

**Stuck in review / pending**

- Confirm you finished Meet / liveness if that stage is open
- Refresh Identity Verification and check email (including spam)

**Declined package**

- Read the rejection reason carefully
- Replace only the flagged files unless instructed otherwise
- Ensure names match your profile and other IDs
