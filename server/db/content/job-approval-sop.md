# Job Approval SOP

**SOP-JOB-002**

## Purpose

Approve compliant job posts after Job Review checks pass.

## Step 1 — Confirm review checklist complete

### Check

- Content policy pass
- No open fraud flags

### Expected result

Safe to approve.

## Step 2 — Approve in system

### Expected result

Job live / visible per product rules; note recorded if UI requires.

## DO

- Approve only after review
- Keep audit trail

## DON'T

- Bulk-approve without sampling high-risk employers

## Escalate when

- Approve fails with entitlement / system errors
