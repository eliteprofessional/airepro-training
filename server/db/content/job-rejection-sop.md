# Job Rejection SOP

**SOP-JOB-003**

## Purpose

Reject non-compliant jobs with clear, actionable reasons for the employer.

## Step 1 — Select reason

### Check

- Reason matches evidence (prohibited content, incomplete, scam signals, etc.)

### Expected result

Employer can remediate or understands permanent reject.

## Step 2 — Reject

### Expected result

Job not live; reason stored.

## DO

- Prefer structured reasons
- Escalate scam patterns instead of soft rejects when fraud is likely

## DON'T

- Use vague “policy violation” when a specific reason exists

## Escalate when

- Organized scam / trafficking risk
- Legal hold requests
