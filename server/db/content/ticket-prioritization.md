# Ticket Prioritization

## Purpose

Assign priority using impact and urgency when working Help and Support / Contact Management queues.

## Suggested priority model (illustrative — not final SLA)

| Priority | Examples | Suggested first response target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P1 | Account lockout blocking work; safety; active payment loss in progress | Immediate / highest |
| P2 | Payment mismatch; IDV blocking applications; payout stuck | Same business day |
| P3 | How-to, non-blocking profile issues | Next business day |
| P4 | Feedback / low impact | Backlog |

## Checklist

- [ ] Impact to money, access, or safety?
- [ ] Multiple users / outage signal?
- [ ] Can customer work around?
- [ ] Correct queue (Support vs Payments vs IDV vs TNS)?

## DO

- Raise priority when money movement is disputed
- Re-prioritize when new facts appear

## DON'T

- Treat every angry tone as P1 without impact
- Invent SLA hours as contractual promises

## Escalate when

- P1 safety / fraud
- Systemic outage suspected
