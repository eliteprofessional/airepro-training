# Job Review SOP

**SOP-JOB-001**

## Purpose

Review employer job posts for policy compliance before approval.

## When to use

Job appears in a review / moderation queue (confirm live OBO/Hire path for your environment).

## Before you start

- Know client package **job-post limits** and feature gates (see Hire APPROD-32 style limits — clients may be blocked from posting when over plan)
- Use synthetic examples in training

## Step 1 — Content review

### Check

- Title and description not prohibited (scams, illegal work, discriminatory content)
- Compensation / location / type coherent
- Employer signals not obviously fraudulent

### Expected result

Pass → approval path; fail → rejection path; unsure → escalate.

## Step 2 — Plan / entitlement awareness

### Check

- If posting fails for the client, may be package limit rather than content reject

### Expected result

Do not “approve” around entitlement errors — route to Support/Sales as appropriate.

## DO

- Document reject reasons clearly for employers
- Escalate scam patterns to fraud

## DON'T

- Approve empty or bait posts
- Invent legal advice in notes

## Escalate when

- Suspected scam hiring / phishing jobs
- Repeated policy evasion by same employer
